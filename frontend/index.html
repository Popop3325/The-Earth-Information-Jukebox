The planet makes sound. Listen to how it changes.

We turn NASA satellite data on Earth's vegetation (NDVI, December 2023 – June 2026) into sound. Snow becomes a winter wind, green land becomes birds and bells, spring thaw becomes dripping water — so you can hear the seasons change across a continent, even with your eyes closed.

About this project

Data

NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, SVS 5544: Near Real-Time NDVI from the VIIRS instrument on Suomi NPP, 27 Dec 2023 – 13 Jun 2026, one frame per day.

Snow, bare land and green land are estimated from the colours of each frame of the video.

How it sounds

  • Snow → winter wind (howling)
  • Green land → birds and wind chimes
  • Melting snow → dripping water
  • Autumn → dry wind and wooden clicks
  • Direction of change → a quiet flute rises or falls

Accessibility

The game can be played without a screen: press Space to let nature answer, or Enter to hear everything. Works with screen readers; every action has a keyboard shortcut.

Disclaimer

This is an artistic sonification for exploring and learning, not a scientific measurement. Values are approximated from video colours; near-real-time data can contain clouds and gaps.
